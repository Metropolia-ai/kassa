Title: Kassa: two AI bookkeepers settle an invoice on PayPal (PayPal AI Hackathon 2026)

Description:
Kassa is an AI bookkeeper for small companies. It reads each supplier invoice, checks it against what was agreed,
asks the supplier's bookkeeper about the difference, and pays through PayPal within the limits its owner set.
When both companies run Kassa, the two bookkeepers settle the invoice between themselves; people are only
brought in when the rules say so.

Shown in the video (hosted demo, PayPal sandbox, nothing mocked in post):
0:00 The job nobody has time for
0:22 Your rules decide (agent limits)
0:42 Two bookkeepers, one invoice: a real PayPal invoice, a question, an answer from the work log, a PayPal Payout, payment recorded
1:23 A duplicate, caught: the supplier's old system re-sends the invoice; it is recognised and withdrawn
1:44 People only when it matters: a bill outside the limits becomes one decision card and one click
2:22 Every decision, with its reason: ask Kassa why something was paid
2:41 What is live and what is seeded

Built with PayPal Invoicing v2, PayPal Payouts, the invoice record-payment API, FastAPI and an LLM via OpenRouter.
