Kassa × Kassa demo

This demo runs a real language model and two PayPal sandbox accounts. Enter the access key you were given. It stays in this browser.

What you are looking at

Two small companies. Each runs Kassa, an AI bookkeeper with its own PayPal account. Pixelforge Oy does render work for Frappua. When Pixelforge's bookkeeper sends an invoice, Frappua's bookkeeper checks it against the purchase order, and the two settle the difference between themselves on PayPal: invoice, query, answer, payout, receipt.

Each owner sets how far its agent may go alone. Inside those limits nobody is interrupted. Beyond them, the owner gets a card with both agents' summaries and decides in one click. The agents then finish the paperwork.

  1. Pick a scenario. The first one settles without a person. The second stops at the owners' limits and asks you. The third is a duplicate invoice: refused and withdrawn, nobody looks.
  2. Watch the wire. Every message is written by the model, but every decision is made by the owner's policy: the amounts, what may be accepted, when to ask.
  3. Change the limits on either side and run the same scenario again. A looser tolerance means fewer questions; a higher floor means more escalations.
  4. Check PayPal. Invoice ids, payout batch ids and statuses under each message are real sandbox objects.

Everything runs in the PayPal sandbox: no real money moves. "Start over" clears the demo data on both sides.
